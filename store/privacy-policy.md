# Privacy Policy — The Modality

_Last updated: 12 June 2026_

The Modality ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

The Modality does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything
you do in the app stays on your device.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other
personal data. We do not track your behavior across apps or websites. We do not
use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

Your learning progress — which makams and modes you've explored, quiz results,
and preferences — is stored locally on your device only, using the operating
system's secure storage. It never leaves your device, and it is removed if you
delete the app.

## Content loaded from third parties

To show recordings and images, the app loads content from third-party services:

- **YouTube** — recordings are shown using YouTube's embedded player. When a
  video loads, YouTube (Google) may collect data as described in Google's
  Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This happens only when
  you open a screen containing a video.
- **Wikimedia Commons / Wikipedia** — musician portrait images are loaded from
  Wikimedia. Loading an image involves a standard web request to Wikimedia's
  servers.

We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of these
services. No personal information about you is sent to them by the app; only the
standard network request needed to load the content is made.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with
a new "last updated" date.
